BOUNTY LEDGER - INSTALL (early test build)

1. Close WoW, or be ready to /reload.
2. Unzip this file. You'll get a folder named "BountyLedger".
3. Move the BountyLedger folder into your addons folder:
     Beta:  World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns\
   (At launch, use the AddOns folder inside the Forever game folder.)
   The final path should end in ...\Interface\AddOns\BountyLedger\BountyLedger.toc
   If you get ...\AddOns\BountyLedger\BountyLedger\..., move the inner folder up one level.
4. Start the game. At character select, click AddOns and make sure Bounty Ledger is checked.

USING IT
  /bounty                 open the board
  /bounty post 5 note     post a 5 gold bounty on your current target (an enemy player)
  /bounty test            show the BOUNTY CLAIMED banner
  /bounty toasts | sound  turn popups or sounds on or off

HOW IT WORKS
  - Post a bounty on an enemy-faction player. Everyone on your faction running the addon sees it.
  - Get the killing blow on a wanted player in the open world and the claim is recorded automatically.
    Battleground and dungeon kills don't count.
  - The poster pays the hunter by normal trade. Both addons record the trade, and the payment shows as Verified.
  - Every player has a public record of bounties paid and unpaid.

This is an early test build. Please report problems and red error popups (screenshots help) to Colton.
